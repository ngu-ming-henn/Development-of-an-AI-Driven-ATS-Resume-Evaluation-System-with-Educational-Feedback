Find the story behind your score.

Resume draft

Turn your feedback into an ATS-ready draft.

After the analysis, the report builds a clean resume draft from your own evidence for the role you chose. You review it, then download it as a PDF.

  • ✓
    Built from your resume

    Your name, contact details, skills, projects and education stay in their own sections.

  • ✓
    A layout ATS can read

    One column and standard headings, without tables, icons or photos.

  • ✓
    Advice stays in the report

    Missing keywords are listed as tips, never added as experience you do not have.

Privacy Policy

Data use for UTS CS Career Navigator

We use account details and resume content to provide this educational tool.

Uploaded resumes are processed to generate ATS feedback, keyword matching, and improvement suggestions.

Your account stores your name, email, and a password hash. Saved analysis records include text extracted from your resume and a copy of the file you upload. Administrators can view saved reports and uploaded files. Deleting a report or clearing your history removes both.

Do not upload confidential or third-party information without permission. This tool is for educational feedback, so review its suggestions before using them in an application.

Terms of Service

Guidelines for using the UTS CS career tool

This tool provides resume feedback for UTS Computer Science students and is intended as guidance only.

Users are responsible for the accuracy of uploaded resumes and selected target roles.

Do not upload confidential or third-party material unless you have permission to do so.

Research Credits

Methods used in the project

The app uses PDF extraction, optional image OCR, TF-IDF similarity scoring, and simple rule-based feedback generation.

The layout and workflows were shaped to support resume preparation for UTS Computer Science students.

About This Project

Built for students learning to improve a resume

This educational project was developed at the University of Technology Sarawak (UTS), Malaysia.

It offers guidance for resume preparation. It is not an official university service or an employer's hiring system.
